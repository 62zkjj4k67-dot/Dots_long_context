# Privacy checklist before sharing a modified kit

Run through this before you publish a fork, open a pull request, or send the kit to anyone.

- [ ] No real names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses.
- [ ] No company, client, project, site or case names from your own work.
- [ ] No tenant URLs, cloud folder or file IDs, workspace or project IDs, schedule IDs.
- [ ] No financial, medical, legal or HR details.
- [ ] No passwords, API keys, tokens or connection strings, even expired ones.
- [ ] No populated records (`tasks.json`, `reconciliation.json`, `work_updates.jsonl`, `AREA.md`, `PROJECT.md`), conversation exports or screenshots.
- [ ] Examples use obviously fictitious data.
- [ ] Archive metadata checked: no stray files, hidden folders or author fields in any ZIP you publish.
- [ ] A quick search of the whole tree for `@`, `http`, your surname and your employer's name returns nothing unexpected.

Publish the sanitized kit only, never the folder where your own records live.
